# 🚀 AI Job Impact Analyzer
This is a Streamlit web app that allows users to check whether their job tasks are Replaced by AI or Not Replaced by AI based on predefined classifications.

📽 Video Demo
📌 
🌟 Features
✅ Enter a job role (e.g., "Actor")
✅ Get a list of tasks associated with that role
✅ See AI impact classification:

✅ Augmented/Replaced by AI
❌ Not Replaced by AI
✅ Q&A Section – Ask questions about AI's impact on the role


💡 How It Works
The user enters a job role in the input box.
The app fetches a list of associated tasks.
Each task is labeled as Replaced by AI ✅ or Not Replaced by AI ❌.
Users can ask questions about AI's impact on their role.
